1. Introduction & System Requirements

PARE Platform Overview

The PARE System is a cloud-hosted web application designed to streamline bus fleet management, process instant ticket bookings, track driver remittances, and provide real-time GPS tracking for daily commuters. You do not need to download or install any software.

System Requirements
  • Device: Any modern smartphone, tablet, or computer.
  • Browser: An up-to-date web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Mozilla Firefox).
  • Permissions: You must allow "Location/GPS Tracking" when prompted to use the Live Map.

2. Account Registration & Login

auth/register.php | auth/login.php

2.1 Registering as a Passenger

  1. On the login screen, click the Register here link.
  2. Select your account type: Regular Account (instant access) or Discount Account (requires ID).
  3. If you selected Discount Account, follow the prompts to capture or upload a clear photo of your valid ID (Senior, PWD, Student).
  4. Fill in your personal details (Name, Address, Emergency Contact).
  5. Retrieve the 6-digit verification code sent to your email and enter it to verify your account.
Discount Approval Process
Discount Accounts will remain in a "Pending Approval" state until an Administrator reviews your uploaded ID. You cannot use the discount until approved.

2.2 Logging In

  1. Navigate to the main homepage.
  2. Select your role by clicking either the Passenger or Driver tab.
  3. Enter your Email Address (or ID Number) and Password, then click Sign In.
  4. If prompted with Two-Step Verification (MFA), check your email for the OTP and enter it to proceed.

3. Passenger Portal

passenger/dashboard.php | passenger/map.php

The Passenger Portal allows commuters to view active buses in real-time, review past trips, and manage their profile.

3.1 Tracking a Live Bus

  1. From the navigation sidebar, click the Live Map tab.
  2. Observe the map interface; active buses are indicated by pulsing orange dots.
  3. Click on any orange dot to view the specific bus body number and its current location.

3.2 Viewing Trip History

Click the My Rides tab to review your historical log of completed trips. You will see the date, time, assigned bus number, and exact fare deducted.

4. Driver Portal

driver/dashboard_v2.php | driver/earnings.php

The Driver Portal is optimized for mobile devices to allow drivers to operate the system effortlessly while in the vehicle.

4.1 Starting and Ending a Trip

  1. Upon logging in, you will be directed to the Drive dashboard.
  2. Select your route for the current shift.
  3. Click the Start Trip button to activate your GPS location for passengers.
  4. When you reach the final terminal, click the End Trip button to finalize the shift log.
Scanning Passenger Tickets
When passengers board, use the built-in scanner to scan passenger tickets/QR codes. The system will automatically calculate and deduct the fare based on your GPS distance.

5. Admin Portal

admin/dashboard.php | admin/fare_settings.php

The Admin Portal is the central command center for fleet operators to monitor revenue and configure system-wide rules.

5.1 Configuring Fare Settings

  1. From the navigation menu, click the Fare Settings tab.
  2. Enter the Base Fare (for the first 4 kilometers) and the Succeeding Km Rate.
  3. Under Discounts, enter the percentage discount (e.g., 20%) for Seniors, PWDs, and Students.
  4. Click the Save Changes button.

5.2 Approving Discount Applications

  1. Click the Passengers tab in the navigation menu.
  2. Look for accounts marked with a Pending Approval badge.
  3. Click the View ID button to review the scanned document.
  4. Click Approve if the ID is valid, or Reject if the ID is illegible or invalid.

6. Troubleshooting & FAQs

Common Support Scenarios

6.1 Live Map Issues

The Live Map is not showing any buses. What should I do?
Ensure that you have granted Location/GPS permissions to your browser. If you are a passenger, it is also possible that no drivers have clicked the Start Trip button yet for their shift.

6.2 Account Recovery

I forgot my password. How can I regain access?
On the login screen, click the Forgot password? link. Enter your registered email address and click Send Link. Check your inbox for a secure link to reset your password.

6.3 Pending Status

Why is my Passenger account still "Pending Approval"?
You likely registered for a Discount Account. You cannot access your QR ticket until an Administrator manually reviews and approves the ID you uploaded.
