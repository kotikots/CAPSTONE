PARE System: User Manual

Passenger and Revenue Monitoring System

Welcome to the PARE System. This web-based platform is designed to provide timely bus tracking, instant ticketing, and seamless fare collection — all unified in one seamless experience.

This manual serves as your comprehensive guide to navigating and utilizing the three core portals of the system: Passenger, Driver, and Admin.

1. Introduction & System Requirements

The PARE System is a cloud-hosted web application, meaning you do not need to download or install any software to use it.

System Requirements
  • Device: Any modern smartphone, tablet, or computer.
  • Browser: An up-to-date web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Mozilla Firefox).
  • Internet: A stable internet connection is required.
  • Permissions: You must allow "Location/GPS Tracking" when prompted by your browser to use the Live Map functionality.

2. Account Registration & Login

Registering as a Passenger

  1. Go to the main homepage and click Register here.
  2. Select your account type:
    • Regular Account: Standard fare. Quick sign-up with immediate access.
    • Discount Account: For Seniors, PWDs, and Students. Requires ID scanning.
  3. If you select Discount Account, you will be prompted to take a photo of your valid ID. The system's OCR (Optical Character Recognition) will automatically extract your details.
  4. Verify your email address via the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your inbox.
  5. Complete your profile details and submit.
Note on Discounts Regular accounts are granted instant access. Discount accounts will be placed in a Pending Approval state until an Admin reviews and approves the uploaded ID.

Logging In

  1. Go to the main homepage.
  2. Select your role tab: Passenger or Driver (Admins log in using the same form).
  3. Enter your Email Address (or ID Number) and Password.
  4. Click Sign In.
Two-Step Verification (MFA) Administrators and staff with enhanced privileges will be prompted to enter a 6-digit verification code sent to their email to protect sensitive system data.

3. Passenger Portal

The Passenger Portal allows commuters to view bus locations, track their own rides, and manage their profiles.

  • Dashboard: Gives you a quick overview of your account, your discount status, and quick links to your most used features.
  • Live Map: Displays a real-time GPS map. Active buses are shown as pulsing orange dots. You can tap on a bus to see its body number and current route.
  • My Rides: A historical log of all your completed trips, including the date, time, bus number, and exact fare paid.
  • My Profile: Update your contact number, address, and emergency contact details. If you have a discount account, your ID photo and status (Active/Pending) will be displayed here.

4. Driver Portal

The Driver Portal is optimized for mobile devices, allowing drivers to operate the system right from the driver's seat.

  • Drive (Dashboard):
    • To begin a shift, select your route and click Start Trip.
    • This activates your bus on the Live Map for passengers to see.
    • Once you arrive at the final destination, click End Trip to finalize the log.
  • QR Code Scanning: When passengers board, drivers can utilize the built-in scanner to scan passenger tickets/QR codes to instantly process fare deduction based on GPS distance.
  • Lists (Passengers Today): View a manifest of all passengers who have boarded your bus during the current shift.
  • Earnings: Track your daily cash collected, total passenger count, and see your historical remittance records.

5. Admin Portal

The Admin Portal is the central command center for fleet operators and management.

  • Overview (Dashboard): A high-level analytics view showing today's total revenue, total passengers, and currently active buses on the road.
  • Trip Logs: A detailed list of all trips made by the fleet, including timestamps and driver assignments.
  • Reports & Export: Generate detailed financial and operational reports. Use the Export to CSV button to download the data into Excel for accounting.
  • Fare Settings: Configure the financial rules of the system:
    • Base Fare (First N kilometers)
    • Succeeding per-kilometer rate
    • Discount percentages for Seniors, PWDs, and Students.
  • Passenger Management: View all registered passengers. Review pending Discount Account applications and click Approve or Archive based on their uploaded ID.
  • Fleet Management (Buses & Drivers): Register new buses (assigning plate numbers and body numbers) and create new driver accounts.
  • Security Logs: An audit trail showing system logins, failed attempts, and administrative actions to ensure complete accountability.

6. Troubleshooting & FAQs

The Live Map is not showing any buses! This usually means no drivers have clicked "Start Trip" on their end, or they have lost internet connection. Buses only appear on the map when they are actively on a route.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I get back in?
A: On the login screen, click Forgot password?. Enter your registered email address, and the system will send you a secure link to create a new password.

Q: My discount account says "Pending Approval". Can I still ride?
A: You cannot access your QR ticket until an Admin approves your account. Please wait for an approval email, or contact management to expedite the process.

Q: How do I change my assigned bus as a driver?
A: Drivers cannot change their own bus assignments. If you are driving a different bus today, an Administrator must update your assignment in the Buses tab of the Admin Portal.
